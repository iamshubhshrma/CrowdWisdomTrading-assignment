Method

  • Alignment: post timestamps → UTC → exchange local time; a post belongs to the session whose close it precedes, so features for day d are known at d's close and predict d+1. Posts are also snapped to the hourly bar they fall in.
  • Sentiment: multilingual lexicon for Cantonese / Korean trader slang + English news, with negation handling; weighted by engagement (likes, replies, views) and author reach.
  • Model: ridge on technicals over 1y, plus a ridge on sentiment features fit to its residuals over the 10-day window; refit at every step of an expanding walk-forward, benchmarked against a random walk.
  • Levels: clustered 5-bar swing pivots + volume-profile nodes + floor pivots.
  • Position: composite of social (45%), news (20%) and model (35%) scores; stop beyond the nearest level, target at the next level, only taken if reward:risk ≥ 1.

Research output, not investment advice.
